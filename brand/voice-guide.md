# Bluewater Brand Voice Guide

## Tone

Apple-inspired. Clean, confident, premium — but approachable. Every sentence should feel intentional. Nothing wasted.

## Headlines

- Always end with a **period.** ("Your employees deserve great water.")
- **Sentence case only** — capitalize the first word, lowercase everything else
  - Correct: "The last plastic bottle your office ever orders."
  - Wrong: "The Last Plastic Bottle Your Office Ever Orders."
  - Wrong: "THE LAST PLASTIC BOTTLE YOUR OFFICE EVER ORDERS"
- Keep headlines short. One clear thought per headline.

## Body copy

- Short sentences. Active voice.
- Lead with outcomes, not features. Say what it does for the reader, not what it is.
- No jargon. No filler. No "leverage", "synergy", "cutting-edge", or "world-class".
- If a sentence doesn't earn its place, cut it.

## Examples

| Instead of | Write |
|-----------|-------|
| "Our state-of-the-art SuperiorOsmosis technology delivers industry-leading purification" | "Purified to 99.7%. Every drop." |
| "Leveraging Swedish mineral enrichment capabilities" | "Blended with Swedish mountain minerals." |
| "We provide a comprehensive hydration solution for modern office environments" | "Great water for your office. Still, sparkling, perfectly chilled." |
